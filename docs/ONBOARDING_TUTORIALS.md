# Guided Setup & Complete Tutorial Academy

## Three entry modes
**Quick Start** (2 minutes, skippable), **Guided First Transaction** (interactive), **Learn Tong Ledger** (8 full lessons with a searchable glossary). Demo mode uses fictitious data in a separate ephemeral store and is never merged with personal entries. No account or online banking password required.

## New-user steps
1. Welcome — “Your Money. Your Rules.” Explain manual offline-first system and privacy.
2. Choose display currency (AUD default), language, optional app lock.
3. Add first bank manually: select Commonwealth Bank or type a custom institution; bank connectivity NOT required.
4. Add Salary, Savings and Household Expenses accounts with their own opening amounts and dates.
5. Add one card from the bank; enter limit, current amount owed and payment due date. Explain available credit is NOT cash.
6. Add a loan under a bank or **independent company**; choose Car/House/Personal/Other and enter repayments/frequency.
7. Add an asset and its valuation as of a date. Link any existing loan without double-counting it.
8. Add payer/source of salary, business, freelance or other income; weekly/fortnightly/monthly/irregular schedule, preferred bank account.
9. Log first expense: select one bank account or card, choose category and amount, confirm preview and save.
10. Select 1 of 30 actual pre-built budget PLANs; preview category amounts; customise and save a copy.
11. Review Home dashboard, net worth, safe-to-spend estimates and upcoming bills.
12. Open full Academy or exit; revisit help in More at any time.

## Academy lessons
- L1: What is a ledger, and why a budget is not cash.
- L2: Multiple banks, accounts and custom institutions.
- L3: Credit limits, outstanding debt and repayments without double-counting.
- L4: Loans, interest vs principal, lenders unrelated to banks.
- L5: Assets and dated valuations, salary/business/irregular income.
- L6: Transfers, refunds, expense categorisation, recurring payments.
- L7: 30 ready-made budget plans, custom categories, fixed/percent allocation, rollover.
- L8: Privacy, backups, export, settings, calculations and troubleshooting.

## Tutorial acceptance criteria
First-run can be completed without providing bank credentials or login. Back/Skip/Resume works after app restart (once persistence is implemented). All 30 templates may be previewed without modifying budgets. Demo content never becomes actual personal finance. Include TalkBack labels, captions, readable illustrations, low-motion animations and Punjabi/Hindi translation-ready strings.
